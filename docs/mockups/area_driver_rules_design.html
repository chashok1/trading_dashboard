Area Driver Rules — design

For each market area (Gold, Silver, Oil, Bitcoin, Stocks…), read what drives it and whether those drivers are helping or hurting. Then flag every stock you hold or are about to buy in that area. Built only on data already in your database. All numbers shown are illustrative.

Five verdicts (plus Quiet), one vocabulary everywhere

VerdictMeansWhat you do with it
▼ HeadwindThe drivers are working against this areaThink twice before buying; consider trimming
▲ TailwindThe drivers support this areaBuy signals in this area are confirmed
◆ TurningA driver just flipped (within 5 days) and price is not stretchedPut it on watch; this is often the early entry
⚠ StretchedGood trend, but price is at the top of its range or vol is elevatedDon't chase; wait for a pullback
○ UnclearThe usual driver link is broken (e.g. gold no longer moves against the dollar) or data is staleIgnore driver rules for now and trust price
— QuietNo rule firesNothing to do (silence is the default)

Like your other advisory warnings, this never changes a Final Call. It adds a reason to your existing Action-icon caution or confirm list. A rule can be promoted to influence actions only after it proves itself (section 7).

Who gets a warning (keeps it quiet)

  • Holdings in a Headwind or Stretched area → caution on the Action icon, and counted in "$ in headwind".
  • Buy candidates (Final Call is a buy) in a Headwind area → caution. In a Tailwind area → confirm.
  • Everything else → no warning. The rest of the universe stays silent.
  • One line per stock per area. If several rules fire, the strongest verdict wins and the others show on hover.

5Architecture — extend, don't add a fifth silo

Today, "market condition → my stocks" logic lives in four places that don't talk to each other: the cross-asset rule engine (1 gold rule), market patterns (8 one-day shocks), Risk Dial gauges (sizing), and hard-coded JS warnings in _signalReasons. This design generalizes the cross-asset engine into the single home for standing driver rules, and reuses the exposure map the gauges and patterns already share.

7Noise control & safety

Keeping it quiet

  • Silence default: quiet areas collapse into one "+N quiet" link and generate no warnings.
  • Holdings + buy candidates only get warnings (section 4).
  • One line per stock per area; other fired rules show on hover.
  • "New" dot for 3 days after a verdict changes, so a flip is noticed without an alert.
  • Optional 2-day confirmation before a verdict flips (avoids one-day whipsaw). Off by default; a ref_settings switch.

Safety rails

  • Advisory only. mode='advisory' never touches consolidated_action / trig_action (cockpit rule C4).
  • Promotion gate: a rule may switch to mode='actionable' only when its scorecard is positive on ≥ 30 samples (your TASK_147 rule). It then folds in through the existing cross-asset path at low priority.
  • One zone definition: the derive and the rails must call the same vol-zone helper, so a tile never says "calm" while the rail says "chop".
  • Stale data → Unclear, not wrong: a leg with missing or stale input makes the area Unclear and posts one toolbar warning, rather than silently reading "quiet".
  • Read text describes, never explains: "Dollar ▲ and 10Y ▲ while Gold ▼", never invented causes (same rule as ref_market_pattern.read_text).
