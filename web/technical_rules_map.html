Technical rules flow

How one stock moves through the checks to a final action. The left side reads the Trade and Trend lines. The right side reads where price sits between LRR and TRR, and only runs when the bands are rising. Whether the stock is above both lines or below either one decides the strong or the weak version of a Step 3 action.

Percentages are the share of the way up from LRR (0%) to TRR (100%). Zone 0 and -1 describe the day's low against LRR. Rules approved 2026-10-25 against 163 trading days (Feb to Oct 2026). Bounce exception, rising dip (Buy Some) and neutral dip are unchanged Step 1 cases not drawn here.
